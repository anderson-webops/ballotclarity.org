# Polk County, Iowa candidate automation

The county's [election index](https://www.polkcountyiowa.gov/county-auditor/election/)
offers a dated [November 3 voter-information page](https://www.polkcountyiowa.gov/county-auditor/election/news-and-press-releases/general-election-november-3-2026-voter-information/).
Its complete eight-page candidate list and explicitly linked Des Moines 1 sample
supply 87 normalized candidate/office records from 89 printed names. Two governor
and lieutenant-governor pairs are established by the ballot's printed teams,
not inferred from matching parties. A different precinct's sample is not assigned
to users or used to infer their local eligibility.

## Factual scope

- Eighteen statewide records, two congressional candidates, seven Senate
  candidates, 26 House candidates and 34 local/regional records.
- Literal labels: 23 Republican, 34 Democratic, one Libertarian and thirteen
  explicitly nonpartisan choices. Sixteen judicial retention names have no
  inferred party. Three Court of Appeals retention questions are statewide;
  seven District 5C judges and six associate judges remain regional.
- Eleven explicit `(No candidate)` party rows remain nonperson references.
  A missing party candidate does not establish an unopposed outcome.
- Hospital and conservation offices retain the printed vote-for-two limits;
  agricultural extension retains vote-for-five. The separately printed vacancy
  remains distinct without an invented term length.
- Emails are excluded. No contact address, telephone, inferred campaign website,
  incumbency, nomination or election result is added.

All eight candidate pages, six notice pages and the sample's first page were
rendered for review. PDF creation metadata is not treated as a publication date.
The linked notice was acquired separately and supplies one statewide amendment
and three local measure references; it is not candidate-pairing evidence or
approval to publish interpreted native measures.

## Access and repeat collection

Normal pinned HTTPS succeeds with zero redirects. The exact robots endpoint
returns 404, meaning no robots document was offered, not a 403 denial or a grant
of blanket reuse rights. The initial text-only fetch failure is retained as a
diagnostic. Its status is explicitly bound by the collector; a newly offered
robots policy requires review before further acquisition.

The linked accessibility statement and Human Resources internal-policy index
were reviewed. The computer policy concerns county-owned equipment and employee
Internet/email use, not website scraping permission. Both content regions and
the legal-link/footer inventory are pinned. Copyright is retained, with only its
year normalized for fingerprint comparison. An exactly labeled county dog-license
application with a matching year/path is distinguished from website-use terms;
new or differently labeled legal links still stop collection. No authored
arguments, photos, statement prose or full pamphlet analysis is republished.

`ia-polk-2026` adds a reusable family using explicit publisher-offered dates,
validated page headings, the full candidate PDF and the first listed precinct's
sample. Published PDF links are part of the external catalog, not immutable
annual constants. Their changes invalidate the import cache. A clearly stated
future pending page remains pending until both publications are offered; an
accepted unexpired publication disappearing holds its catalog for review.

The existing bounded `STATE_BALLOT_PDFTOTEXT` worker capability is used for
collection only. Accepted facts remain externally stored and readable without
the converter. Normal reads preserve source hashes and capture clocks; failed
acquisition does not replace durable data. Existing state/source allowlists,
publisher holds and approval boundaries remain unchanged.

Current evidence concerns 2026. Future presidential/pending/link-update tests are
synthetic compatibility checks, not future candidate evidence. The registry has
64 source definitions and 31 reusable families across 25 states. This does not
establish complete county, statewide or personalized ballot coverage.

## Future county-office compatibility

The county's [2024 Johnston 4 sample](https://www.polkcountyiowa.gov/media/5q0hilnv/johnston-4.pdf)
prints county auditor and sheriff races. The collector now accepts those office
labels alongside treasurer, recorder, attorney and district supervisors, only
inside the county-office section. Unknown or misplaced offices still require
review. This changes recognized published labels, not an inferred election
calendar or future candidate roster.

The synthetic presidential-cycle example includes both auditor and sheriff.
Its pending-to-available import, exact durable restart and unchanged cached
capture time verify together. All current 2026 facts remain unchanged.
