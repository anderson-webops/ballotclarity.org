# Everett recurring candidate collection

The independent [City of Everett election library](https://cityofeverett.com/city-hall/departments/elections-department/)
offers three November 3, 2026 state-election sample ballots. A fresh normally
validated TLS acquisition retained identical original PDF hashes and all 24
previously reviewed candidate/ticket records, including their original fields,
incumbency captions, three joint tickets and all three ward/precinct styles.
The original 66-occurrence review remains in the [native preparation audit](2026-11-06-everett-native-preparation.md).

## Discovery and policy

The bounded collector reads robots rules before the election index. The page's
current-document heading, table configuration, public nonce and read-only action
identify its public Document Library endpoint. The collector uses the offered
100-row page size, checks consistent totals across every page, and admits at most
200 library entries and 20 distinct sample documents. Each serial request waits
at least twenty seconds. Publisher redirects, access challenges, TLS failures,
changed policy surfaces, unknown table schemas and ambiguous publication dates
stop acquisition.

The enrolled policy surface retains the footer and policy-link inventory. Only
the known copyright-year counter is normalized; changed terms and additional
policy links still require review. No explicit reuse license was established.
Raw publisher documents remain private evidence; the application retains
normalized public candidate facts and their attribution rather than republishing
the PDFs. Existing Massachusetts state, Cambridge and Somerville holds remain.

Sample titles, publication years, exact offered URLs and dates printed inside
each native ballot bind discovery. Future sources receive date-bound IDs without
probing guessed PDFs. An empty current library creates no election date. Every
sample receives the existing independent XML/SVG outline and placement check.
The current native reader supports the reviewed twelve-office, single-candidate-
page layout; other layouts and newly encountered glyphs require review. Synthetic
2028, 2030 and 2032 fixtures verify configurable dates and identities, not actual
coverage of those elections.

## Retained state and public reads

`ma-everett-2026` retains its previous manual source as an exact migration
predecessor. Replacing a manual snapshot first preserves its original envelope
in an immutable, hash-named backup. Automatic snapshots bind their offered style
inventory, original document hashes, original retrieval times, first-owner unique
record counts and complete normalized facts. Missing accepted records or styles
fail closed. Public directory, detail and lookup readers use saved data without
publisher requests or native conversion.

Recurring synchronization retains accepted snapshots during acquisition or
persistence failure and acknowledges imports only after durable storage. The
ordinary election-window cutoff remains active; historical records remain
available through explicit source selection. Named legislative districts stay
out of address-specific matching pending a verified crosswalk. Official sample
navigation leads to the city's style chooser instead of selecting one ward's
ballot for every visitor.

## Verification scope

Deterministic tests cover complete pagination, serial pacing, future identities,
literal candidate facts, policy/schema/URL/date/glyph substitutions, accepted-
record disappearance, retained snapshots, and offline public reads. The local
real-document replay also exercises synchronization, repeat/cache, restart,
manual backup, disappearance rejection, outage, persistence failure and recovery.
Its active-window cases use explicitly rebased fixture clocks; the original
captured files and timestamps remain unchanged. Real-clock synchronization
correctly classifies November 2026 as expired and performs no import.

Source validation establishes collector capability. Production data approval,
publication, source enrollment, recurring scheduling and deployment remain
separate acceptance outcomes.
