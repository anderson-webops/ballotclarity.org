## Prepared factual content

- Amendment 1 concerns pretrial bail, not a conviction or sentence. The summary
  retains the proof condition, capital-offense exception, added listed offenses,
  November 3, 2026 cutoff for the 85%-sentence category, recorded judicial reasons
  for granting or denying bail, and the retained habeas-corpus clause. It does not
  say that bail must automatically be denied for every accusation.
- Amendment 2 explicitly concerns a state property tax on real, personal or
  mixed property. No current rate, existing revenue, local-tax abolition or
  automatic new tax after a NO vote is invented.
- Amendment 3 covers criminal and juvenile proceedings, specified upon-request
  qualifications, parole/release participation and safety, protection,
  restitution, nonparty assertion and legislative implementation. The summary
  preserves due process, prosecutorial/court authority, the no-vacatur provision
  and the qualified public-entity cause-of-action/damages limitation.

These are original neutral summaries of printed government ballot facts, not
copied vendor artwork, advocate arguments or independently researched legal
advice. No fiscal estimate, effective date or approval-threshold explanation is
invented from these samples. Detail sections remain ordinary content, not
dropdowns. Statewide coverage does not claim a complete local or personal ballot.
