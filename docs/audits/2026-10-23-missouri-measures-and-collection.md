# Missouri measure-source and connected-guide review

## Scope

Five November 3, 2026 statewide questions are prepared as a separate native
guide: Amendments 3, 6, 7, 8 and Proposition A. August 4 questions on the
same index are excluded. No local races, personalized ballot eligibility or
candidate nominations are inferred. The statewide-only limitation remains
visible on the guide, saved plan and printout.

This is local review work, not a published source release, active data import,
protected deployment, new scheduler or production acceptance. Existing
publication approvals remain independent and false for this packet.

## Evidence and content

The [Secretary of State's certified-measures index](https://www.sos.mo.gov/petitions/2026BallotMeasures)
links the five retained certificates. The original bytes, SHA-256 hashes and
capture times are preserved separately from native preparation metadata.
Issuance dates and retrieval dates are not assumed publication/update dates.

- Amendment 3 covers abortion restrictions and gender-transition procedures
  for minors. Its title names both subjects. Greene County's possible
  unknown tax-revenue increase remains distinct from other entities'
  no-cost/no-savings estimates.
- Amendment 6's 80% rule concerns each chamber referring covered changes to
  voters, not directly enacting those changes. This distinction is verified
  against the separately retained [full initiative text, pages 2 and 3](https://www.sos.mo.gov/CMSImages/Elections/Petitions/2026-106.pdf#page=2),
  rather than inferred from the shorter ballot title. The certification PDF
  contains a court opinion and is not a substitute for that full text.
- Amendment 7's potential tax elimination is conditional on sufficient fund
  earnings. The principal-protection, appropriation-cap and shortfall
  qualifications are not converted into a guaranteed or immediate tax cut.
- Amendment 8 covers sheriff election, duties and removal rules.
- Proposition A concerns approval of the congressional redistricting act
  from the 2025 second extraordinary session. No partisan forecasts or
  address assignments are added.

All titles retain their official amendment/proposition numbers. Yes/No
choices follow the official vote explanations. Only short original factual
summaries and precise attribution are prepared, not complete pamphlets,
graphics or third-party arguments. No blanket open-republication license is
claimed.

The final standalone review packet contains one native draft plus six
prepared guide/workflow/review/provenance/manifest/checksum files. Twelve
provenance CSV rows represent five questions, not twelve questions. Raw
publications are excluded from the seven-member, 18,180-byte packet. Its
SHA-256 is
`e540d3770c715e8da262ca7a76b5f7900a1ffc80c9de89ea56bc3eb27c1ca30f`.
Structural validation has zero blockers; nine independent review warnings
remain, and the package is not marked ready to publish.

## Collection changes

`mo-measure-documents-2026` adds an election-bound, same-host catalog entry
for exactly five reviewed November certificate filename families. The full
initiative text is a manual supplement, not an additional automatic target.
Raw cache and output remain outside source/release trees. Successful
collection does not import or publish content.

The reviewed privacy page uses an outer ASP.NET form and has no HTML `main`
element. An explicit, unique `div#main` policy region can be fingerprinted
without weakening existing main-only review. Missing, duplicated or empty
regions fail closed; scripts are not executed.

Missouri's robots file includes separate SemrushBot and BLEXBot groups.
Support is limited to these explicitly reviewed non-applicable groups. The
wildcard restrictions remain binding for the unchanged importer identity.
Unknown, applicable, merged or changed groups require a new supported review.
Request spacing, conditional validation, policy/link fingerprints, bounded
PDF handling, denial/redirect holds, DNS/TLS checks, locks, failure cooldown,
review expiry and election expiry remain enforced.

Five certificate requests completed through the bounded collector. Source
and compiled replays reuse all five documents with zero publisher requests
and preserve original capture times. The separate full-text supplement has
its own hash and capture time. The existing North Dakota configuration is
unchanged.

## Connected lookup corrections

Previously, opening a verified guide discarded otherwise available district
and current-official results. The client now retains those results only for
the matching published guide/election/state; the homepage still prioritizes
the guide rather than duplicating the results panel. Entered street addresses
remain excluded from browser local storage and page URLs.

The API previously rejected active civic context when a verified guide was
available, leaving a measure-only guide's directories empty. Civic context
now remains available through the existing encrypted HttpOnly/SameSite
cookie and explicit ZIP routes. An explicit guide-area request is not
overridden by an older cookie. This does not publish draft candidates or
promote current officials to verified ballot candidates.

Directory queries and client merging honor explicit ZIP/selection/area
identity. Different guide areas do not reuse the previous location label or
matches. Scoped district aliases reconcile opaque provider identifiers
without double-counting a district or merging House and Senate records.
Sources and profile/detail links remain reachable without content dropdowns.

## Verification boundary

Verification uses retained local source data with explicitly synthetic
geography/publication fixtures, never new public official identities or
actual publication approvals. Historical clocks exercise candidate caches
without advancing their original capture times. The final local acceptance
receipt records native checks, source/compiled parity, API/route population,
keyboard/save/reload/print journeys, rendering/accessibility checks, scoped
local secret review and exact predecessor preservation.

No dependency, clean-install, external scanning-service, production-data,
public activation or national-completeness claim is made by this audit.
The national/state/local collection objective remains incomplete.
