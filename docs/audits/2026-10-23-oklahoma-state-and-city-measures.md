# Oklahoma state questions and Woodward city proposition

## Accepted source evidence

The Woodward County Election Board's current November 3, 2026 index links three
sample PDFs: the general ballot, the general ballot with House District 58, and
the separate City of Woodward proposition. Normal verified downloads on October
23 byte-match the three originals retained on October 21. Earlier candidate
records and their original capture dates are unchanged.

Both general samples print State Questions 845 and 847 on page 2. The city sample
prints an unnumbered hotel-room-tax question on page 1. The source images were
reviewed upright; OCR is not the authority for article numbers, ages, percentages
or the printed vote choices.

The county's robots permits these public paths. Its linked accessibility page
and copyright-bearing footer were reviewed independently of Oklahoma.gov and the
Secretary of State. This work contains original factual summaries, not copied
publisher artwork, website prose or advocate statements. No blanket state-work
public-domain or vendor licensing claim is made.

## Native review data

- SQ 845 explains the Judicial Nominating Commission's appointment, eligibility
  and term changes, including two-year at-large terms and six-year other terms.
  The printed no-fiscal-impact statement is attributed to the ballot, not
  represented as an independent fiscal finding.
- SQ 847 distinguishes valuation-growth caps from tax rates. The 2027 general
  limits and all six senior-homestead income tiers retain their printed values
  and boundaries. No tax-bill savings or public-revenue estimate is invented.
- Woodward's hotel-room-tax draft is city-scoped, not countywide. It preserves
  the question's up-to-10%-of-room-charges wording without inventing a current or
  resulting total rate, effective date, revenue forecast or proposition number.

The exact printed proposal and proposition YES/NO pairs remain distinct through
the existing decision contract. Arbitrary, mixed-subject, partial and reversed
pairs are rejected. No frontend content dropdowns are introduced.

Two jurisdiction-bound guide drafts, citation CSV, review holds, manifests and
checksums use the existing offline preparation contract. They append to the
pinned review assembly without replacing the primary guide or prior added
guides. All new workflows remain draft; coverage metadata remains unknown.

## Holds and limits

Both county samples label SQ 845 as Legislative Referendum 378, while the State
Election Board discovery index identifies it as 379. The original references
are retained. This discrepancy remains an explicit editorial-publication hold,
not a silent correction or a second measure. Its review metadata is not added
as explanatory interface copy.

The Secretary of State's robots disallows its root. No SOS PDF was downloaded
locally and no cached web content, mirror, alternate identity or host is used to
work around that restriction. Oklahoma.gov's copyright page links Tyler terms;
the terms-path robots restriction leaves that separate policy review incomplete.
The state-site index is discovery evidence, not an accepted automatic source.

These are partial measure guides, not complete candidate, judicial-retention or
personal-ballot assignments. Exact city matching, publication gates and official
ballot-confirmation limits remain independent.

## Collection contract

`ok-woodward-documents-2026` adds only the three reviewed county PDF paths. Both
current index markers and all three documents are required. Older questions,
primary samples and cross-host links cannot enter the capture set. Existing
policy fingerprints, request spacing, bounds, cooldowns, local storage and
review-expiry gates remain in effect. Collection does not parse, import, approve
or publish candidate or measure records. The earlier candidate-source adapter is
unchanged.

## Delivery boundary

Prepared review data and raw captures remain outside Git and application release
artifacts. Source verification, editorial approval, data import and production
activation are separate states. This cohort does not authorize publication,
private transfer, active data replacement or scheduled collection.
