# Utah measures and Hawaii publisher-policy review

## Local source changes

- Utah's two November 3 constitutional amendments now have native guide drafts,
  six prepared guide/review outputs and source-page CSV citations. Publication,
  automatic import and primary-guide replacement remain unapproved.
- The retained state pamphlet supplies analysis, fiscal estimates, attributed
  arguments and conditional dates. Utah County's independently authored sample
  ballot, PDF page 2, confirms the printed For/Against labels for both statewide
  questions. Its local candidates and district-specific questions are not
  promoted into statewide coverage.
- Amendment A's possible savings remain **up to $1.4 million every two years**,
  not a guaranteed annual saving. Amendment B's assessment is unquantified,
  not zero. Its November 1 initiative-vote cutoff and November 23 conditional
  effective date remain separate, with their legal interaction unresolved.
- Opposing-argument non-submission for Amendment A is a pamphlet-submission
  fact, not evidence that no public opposition exists. Arguments are attributed
  to the named authors, not presented as official impartial findings.

## Publisher access and collection

[Utah County's index](https://vote.utahcounty.gov/public-notices) links its own
sample ballot and the county's terms/privacy pages. Both publisher hosts have
permissive robots documents. The reviewed terms allow informational use subject
to document-specific restrictions and do not establish a blanket open license.
The research browser could not render the terms page, but the retained normal
pinned-transport capture returned HTTP 200 and its full policy text was reviewed.

The new document catalog entry requires both exact publisher hosts' robots
policies, homepage policy links, terms, privacy, election markers and the exact
observed PDF path. Each host has its own delay, request-completion clock and
permissions. Changed policy links/text, denial, redirects or invalid content
hold the source. Uppercase `.PDF` paths retain PDF content-type and byte checks.
The existing North Dakota, Missouri and New Mexico catalog entries are unchanged.
Collection stores external source material only; it does not approve or import it.

Offline native preparation now accepts pinned PDFs up to 32 MiB, allowing the
30,792,889-byte retained Utah pamphlet. JSON/cache files retain their 12 MiB
default bound, total native-preparation inputs stay within 64 MiB and prepared
outputs remain bounded to 12 MiB each. Network document collection stays at
12 MiB and is not broadened to download the larger state pamphlet automatically.
