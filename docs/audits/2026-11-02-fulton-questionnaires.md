# Fulton candidate questionnaires

The [Buckhead Council of Neighborhoods election index](https://buckheadcouncil.org/election-central-2026/)
offers candidate-provided responses independently of the candidates' campaign
sites. Normal acquisition preserves the index, permissive wildcard robots,
original PDF bytes, hashes and actual capture times. No restricted campaign
website, video, Google document, photograph or personal contact data is collected.

Eight questionnaires provide 24 short, neutral summaries of stated positions:
Eric J. Tatum, Jodi Merriday, Paul Burton, Kevin Abel, Sonya Halpern,
John F. Guest, Jr., Deborah Silcox and Helmut Lucero Love. The three county
questionnaires cover tax allocation districts, property assessments and county
services. The five legislative questionnaires cover housing, public safety and
education. These are attributed statements, not independently established policy
outcomes, verified accomplishments, endorsements or ballot-qualification evidence.

Preparation binds each questionnaire to its exact existing official candidate,
office and election. All 98 original Fulton candidate records remain unchanged.
The source adds policy positions for Tatum and Burton; it does not create
biographies or claim to have resolved the restrictions on their campaign sites.

## Reusable collection

The existing reviewed candidate-statement preparer and recurring collector now
accept explicitly configured, named PDFs as well as HTML. Each PDF has a small
page limit, an exact original byte hash and a meaningful extracted-text hash.
Preparation and reference import both enforce the configured candidate name.
Candidate-provided PDFs cannot be relabeled as official biographies.

The source admits eight exact publication paths with a complete-document count,
current policy review and policy/link fingerprints. Cached repeat preserves the
original capture dates without publisher requests. Changed statements require a
new substantive review while accepted output remains recoverable. Size, page,
identity, policy, expiry, network pacing and persistent-hold safeguards remain.
Later elections require their own reviewed candidates and statements; this
configuration does not infer later rosters or reuse old positions as new ones.

## Holds

Greg Miller's offered PDF identifies Georgia House District 45, while the index
and official Fulton ballot identify District 54. The original capture is retained
for review but is excluded from the configured reference collection.

Otero County, New Mexico publishes a current clerk index with sample-ballot
links. Its reviewed robots groups permit that index and preserve AI-specific
restrictions. Its linked privacy page, however, contains only a heading, confirmed
in both normal HTML and a settled browser rendering. No ballot PDF is acquired
and no automatic source is enrolled pending a meaningful policy review. These
findings do not alter the independent New Mexico source holds.

Roswell Ledger's previously retained terms limit content to personal,
non-commercial use. Reuse review remains incomplete and its Tatum article is
not acquired. Private custody, substantive approval, import, publication and
recurring activation remain separate milestones.
