## Draft content

- Measure 2 replaces open top-four primaries/ranked-choice general elections with
  party primaries/single-choice elections and changes specified campaign-finance
  limits, disclosures, definitions and fines. Its draft retains the revised
  August 28 language, rather than presenting it as an election-method-only proposal.
  The Legislative Affairs Agency's constitutional concerns are attributed and
  enforceability is not independently resolved. Complete clause-by-clause review
  of the longer bill remains open.
- Measure 3 adds “only” to the qualification statute while retaining its four
  existing conditions. The current official language, government analysis and
  one-section proposed text support that limited scope; no new proof, registration
  or disenfranchisement rule is inferred.
- Official initiative cost totals are $2,630,958 and $114,050 respectively. They
  include pre-election petition/hearing work, and not all costs depend on passage.
  The agency minimum excludes specified legal/challenge costs. These totals are
  not presented as annual budgets, household taxes or purely post-passage costs.
