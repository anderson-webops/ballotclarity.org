# Franklin notices and Tuscola ballot questions

## Source boundaries

Tuscola's November 3, 2026 sample contains twelve distinct questions across
307 printed occurrences in seventy two-sided styles. Four state/county
questions recur in every style; eight local questions account for twenty-seven
additional occurrences. Native re-extraction preserves the 191 candidate/ticket
records, original PDF hashes and original collection times. Literal Yes/No
choices and every physical page citation remain attached to each question.

Franklin's independently collected certified-issues table contains fifty-one
notices across five pages. Its purpose, rate and charter summaries remain
**election notices**, with no inferred full ballot wording or vote choices.
Compound tax/bond rows keep their purpose/rate association. Unnumbered rows
remain unnumbered. All seventy-eight retained candidate records match; the
new collection carries its own actual acquisition times and document hashes.

Primary publication indexes:

- [Tuscola County elections](https://www.tuscolacounty.org/elections/)
- [Franklin County 2026 election information](https://www.vote.franklincountyohio.gov/election-info/2026)

## Recurring collection

Both readers follow actual offered documents and printed election dates.
Franklin discovery version two adds the certified-issues document when offered;
version one remains readable for retained two-document evidence. New collection
uses version two, and accepted notices cannot silently disappear through a
legacy replay or missing publication. Tuscola also retains accepted questions
on missing choices, changed wording, contradictory repeated wording or missing
questions. Existing candidate and publisher-policy checks remain in force.

Franklin's native preparation uses bounded maintained Poppler text and SVG
extraction. Printed table rules, word geometry, complete page furniture and an
independent layout word inventory are checked before importing summaries.
Public readers consume stored facts without converters or publisher requests.
Synthetic future-cycle tests establish reusable date/identity handling, rather
than availability or approval of unpublished future ballots.

## Review and recovery

Source labels distinguish notices from ballot questions. Each detail cites its
own offered publication and first applicable physical page; repeated-page
inventories stay in the API rather than becoming long visible link labels.
Question audiences preserve publisher scope and establish no voter eligibility.
The known Columbia Township road proposal's conflicting term wording remains
literal and subject to substantive review.

Source validation and private custody remain separate from substantive approval,
reversible production import/publication, geographic assignment and scheduling.
Earlier runtimes require their retained earlier snapshot and source configuration
for rollback when they cannot read the new question or notice metadata.
