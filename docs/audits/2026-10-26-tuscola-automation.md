# Tuscola County automatic candidate collection

## Accepted records and complete publications

The county's [dated election index](https://www.tuscolacounty.org/elections/)
supports the existing `mi-tuscola-candidates-2026` identity as an automatic
external family. Michigan's separate 716-record state source is unchanged.

Normal collection on October 26 downloaded the actually offered candidate list
and sample booklet. Their original SHA-256 hashes still match the retained
September publications:

- Candidate listing: `59f9f6d1f5e2222df9dd13f9b3f70cf8394b25000227045ce41b84f0525bd720`.
- Sample booklet: `9218c5ad62ee8746740a0cfbdddf57db662ec6c27a64e3852f08321970c4894d`.

All 191 existing records retain every field and ID. The listing's 196 printed
people include five explicit governor/lieutenant-governor tickets; they become
191 choices in 62 populated contests. Three explicit empty contests stay empty.
The printed negative vote limit in one empty Akron partial-term contest remains
an anomaly, not a corrected claim or an invented candidate.

The complete listing has **21 physical pages**, with candidate rows on pages
1–19 and proposal material on pages 19–21. Earlier hand-preparation metadata
recorded nineteen listing pages. That archived packet remains unchanged;
nineteen is not the whole-document count. The new reader validates all 21
sequential page headers, dates and geometry. All 140 paired sample pages are
retained for reconciliation, not interpreted as personalized ballot assignments.

## Access and reuse assessment

Current robots rules permit these public paths and exclude edit, search and
other administrative paths. Collection checks the homepage, election-index and
county-policy inventories, their actual footer, the FOIA documents, enhanced
access policy and computer-use policy before election downloads. A broken legacy
FOIA link has a separately pinned empty 404 response; its working published
counterparts are checked independently. The observed `/policies` canonical
redirect is not followed automatically; the reviewed canonical policy URL is
requested directly. New legal links, changed policies, denials and redirects
require review.

The [enhanced-access policy](https://www.tuscolacounty.org/policies/doc/Enhanced%20Access%20to%20Public%20Records.pdf)
addresses digital inspection/copying, retained ownership and as-is information.
The [computer-use policy](https://www.tuscolacounty.org/policies/doc/Computer%20Resources%20Acceptable%20Use%20Policy.pdf)
governs county workers and equipment. The
[FOIA policy](https://www.tuscolacounty.org/doc/FOIA%20Policy.pdf)
addresses records requests and identifies free website access. None is treated
as a blanket content license. This collector extracts factual candidacy fields
from the county's freely offered publications, not arguments, photographs or
whole-document republication. Household addresses, phones, emails and filing
contacts are excluded from persisted records and derived exports.

Requests use normal pinned public transport, strict size/time bounds, zero
redirects and serial waits of at least eleven seconds. The sample booklet is
11,657,556 bytes, below the existing 12 MiB PDF limit. Native text extraction is
bounded at 100 listing pages, 200 sample pages and 4 MiB per text. The optional
absolute maintained PDF worker is needed for collection, not stored public reads.

## Reusable election and integrity contract

Actually offered November date sections select the two linked publications.
The printed general date and county identity must agree throughout the listing
and on every sample front; sample backs retain the paired-page boundary.
Incomplete future publication sets remain pending. Future tests use synthetic
dates and names, never projected real candidacies or guessed download URLs.

Candidate and party columns reconcile on every page. Wrapped names, including
Piché's accent, remain exact. Every printed person must occur in the complete
sample text under case/spacing/punctuation normalization without accent stripping
or fuzzy identity joins. Joint-ticket members and parties must reconcile with
the sample's governor block. Six printed `(I)` markers remain literal designations,
not inferred incumbency. Explicit withdrawals remain inactive source evidence.

Native IDs reproduce the original reviewed preparation's identity fields.
The first automatic import saves the exact reviewed storage envelope before
replacement. Failed backup or persistence preserves accepted facts. Same-URL
PDF hash changes invalidate caches; unchanged successful imports keep their
original capture clocks. Disappearing accepted people or publications remain
review exceptions. Existing reviewed input and enrichment flows continue to use
the exact legacy source fingerprint rather than relabeling old data as automatic.

Township, village, school, library and judicial jurisdictions remain source labels.
Congressional and legislative matching holds remain effective; only the existing
64 statewide records can contribute to those previews. County source preference
does not establish any voter's complete ballot.

## Verification boundary

Local checks exercise current live collection, all 191 fields/IDs, exact reviewed
recovery, cache reuse, same-URL amendments, pending future sets, persistence failure,
legacy enrichment inputs, converter-independent stored reads and connected
desktop/mobile rendering. Source release, reference receipt, scheduled server
imports and public activation remain separate acceptance events.
