## Access and recurrence

Thirty-six original public robots, index, policy and biography captures retain
their hashes and actual capture dates privately. No challenged source was
retrieved through an alternate identity or access path. The independent current
Porcher site and offered Edwards canonical host were reviewed on their own terms.

sharing the wildcard's directives. Exact ordered layout support preserves every

hold leaves the other ten available. Existing spacing, cache locks, access review,
backoff, content holds and election expiry remain enforced. Batch processing
