# Fulton native question and measure bindings

The retained November 3, 2026 native snapshot contains seven printed questions.
All seven reconcile exactly with the prepared measure records across five
elections: Fulton County, statewide Georgia, Sandy Springs, Palmetto and Fulton
County outside Atlanta. The transportation jurisdiction retains Atlanta's explicit
exclusion and its reference-only matching mode.

The maintained measure preparer can now pin that native snapshot and the exact
selected elections through optional `questionSnapshot` configuration. It checks
complete wording with whitespace-only normalization, printed choice order and
labels, advisory effects, root/contest copies, original document hashes and
capture clocks. Missing or ambiguous questions and changed claims fail before
creating the output directory. Existing plans without this option retain their
output format. See the [preparation contract](../state-measure-guide-preparation.md).

Actual compiled preparation preserves all existing combined-coverage fields.
Repeated reconciliation JSON, CSV, guide and coverage outputs are byte-identical.
The new comparison retains the October 27 native capture and separate original
draft/document clocks. It makes zero publisher requests and refreshes neither.
Future-cycle fixtures change election dates while preserving declared scope.

Printed sample pages 3, 4 and 5 were rendered and reviewed. Page 4 literally
contains “new 10 and replacement schools”; both native question and prepared
measure retain that wording. The comparison neither silently edits a publisher's
text nor interprets it as a verified school count. The stated collection ceiling,
two school-system allocations and Atlanta debt limit remain unaltered.

This verifies wording coverage between retained sources and drafts. Independent
legal/fiscal/rights review, jurisdiction and voter applicability, election results,
publication, import and recurring-host activation remain separate. No existing
snapshot, approval decision or public current/historical selection is changed.
