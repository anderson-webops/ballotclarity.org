stated priorities where available. Generic promises, election appeals, family,
religion, personal contacts, ballot numbers, photographs and uncertain
degree/licensure details are omitted. A position is labeled as a position, not
presented as enacted policy. Third-person promotional announcements retain
candidate-statement authorship; an editor's byline does not make them independently
verified biographies.

not a transcript. Raborn/Roy/McIver/Morris have no invented answers or biographical
