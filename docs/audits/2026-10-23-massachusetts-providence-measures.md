## Native content and scope

city measures to the preceding review draft. It preserves the primary guide,
all 61 existing additional guides and their capture clocks, and all candidate
collections. Both new guide workflows remain drafts; publication, import and
personal-eligibility approval flags remain false.

- Massachusetts Questions 1–8 propose laws. Question 9 concerns an existing
  firearms law: YES keeps it and NO repeals it. Neither direction is inverted.
- Question 4 would start Election Day registration in 2028. It does not change
  registration rules for the November 2026 election.
- Question 5 retains the three-year wage-growth basis and inclusion of surtax
  revenue. Question 6 retains legislative appropriation, the 50%/100% stages,
  and the excluded transit/school transfers. Question 7 retains all three lot
  conditions, not just lot area.

  than describing a blanket possession ban. Its source places age 21 in two

  uncertain actual financing terms remain visible. Question 7 preserves the
  fiduciary-duty exception and does not invent designated countries,
  companies, costs or returns.
- Cambridge's retained nonbinding Question 10 applies only to the Twenty-Fifth
  and Twenty-Ninth Middlesex districts. It is not assigned statewide or to all
  Cambridge residents without the missing exact eligibility mapping.
