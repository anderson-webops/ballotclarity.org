## Independent official county ballot

The [Warren County election page](https://warrencountyclerk.ky.gov/services/elections/2026-general-election-info/)
links the county's own [two-page general-election sample ballot](https://warrencountyclerk.ky.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Warren-2026G.pdf).
It contains the county's local races and their precinct labels, not a mirrored
state guide. Page 1 identifies November 3, 2026; page 2 labels Constitutional
Amendment 1 for all precincts and prints the proposed Kentucky constitutional
text. Both pages and an enlarged question crop were visually reviewed. OCR was
an inspection aid, not a substitute for that review.

The ordinary public HTTPS request returned 200, 1,407,979 bytes, captured at
`2026-10-24T07:30:19.898Z`. SHA-256:
`bc8b45060a38697c801dc67b92450321ffcef065016a04c0e3e7a2d7f58f2959`.
The PDF creation timestamp and upload month are not treated as a publication date.

The draft concerns the governor's pardon and sentence-commutation powers around
**gubernatorial** elections, not every general election. It preserves the proposed
Section 77 period: 60 days before that election through the fifth Tuesday afterward.
The introduction instead describes the endpoint as the governor being sworn in;
independent review must confirm the relationship without inventing calendar dates.
The printed text separately addresses fine/forfeiture remission, public decision
reasons and treason reprieves. Their legal application is not independently resolved.

The data uses original factual prose and exact `YES` / `NO` controls. Full official
text, advocacy, a fiscal estimate, a current-law narrative and an implementation
date are not invented or copied. Current constitutional text, passage requirements,
transition timing, exceptional provisions, rights and publication require separate
independent review. The local candidate names in the PDF are not imported here;
the existing 210-record Kentucky candidate capture remains unchanged.
