## Material qualifications

Portland's minimum funding is equivalent to at least 2% of adopted General Fund
discretionary ongoing expenses, not 2% of the entire city budget. Council may
identify funding from the broader budget. The $16.4 million/$17.0 million figures
are preliminary forecasts for FY 2027-28/FY 2028-29, not fixed future amounts.
The stated operational target is July 2027. The pamphlet reports no explanatory
statement filed for this measure; no missing analysis is invented.

Troutdale's levy is $1.60 per $1,000 of assessed value, not market value. Its
illustrative $428.48 annual tax on $267,800 assessed value differs from the stated
$128.48 net increase after subtracting a $300 annual fee. That net illustration
assumes Council eliminates the current $25 monthly fee, described as Council
intent in the city statement, not an independent guaranteed automatic repeal.
The ballot's warning about property-tax increases greater than 3% is retained.

The police-department question is non-binding. It does not create a department,
authorize taxes/fees/spending, hire personnel or terminate contracted services.
Future Council decisions and public processes would still be necessary. It is
not merged with the levy or represented as a binding legal authorization.

The district questions retain their full identities, principal amounts,
conditional matching funds and estimated tax-rate qualifiers in separate review
data. They are not assigned to all residents of Hillsboro, Troutdale, Portland or
Multnomah County. A reviewed district model and eligibility evidence are still
needed. Burlington Water District is not replaced by another similar district.

## Advisory contract and presentation

`decisionEffect: "advisory"` is an optional explicit annotation with mandatory
supported binary `ballotOptions`. Unknown effects, inferred advisory choices and
non-binary options fail validation in both coverage loading and native
preparation. Absence of the field does not assert that another question is
binding. Existing non-binary advisory holds remain unchanged.

The same non-binding label is visible in summary cards, detail context and saved
or printed plans without a content dropdown. Advisory detail pages omit
legislation structured data. The citation CSV appends `decisionEffect` only when
needed; older preparations retain their prior column layout and byte format.
