# North Dakota statewide measure sources

## Current official evidence

The [Secretary of State's current index](https://www.sos.nd.gov/elections/voter/ballot-measures/measures-ballot)
identifies two statewide measures for November 3, 2026. Ten bounded, spaced,
TLS-verified canonical requests captured that index, its robots policy and three
linked policies, two official ballot explanations, two proposed-text documents
and the school-meal fiscal report. All returned 200 without redirects. No publisher
hold, alternative identity, larger bound, mirror, credentials or challenge route
was used. Local question completeness is not established by this statewide list.

The actual SOS site's linked Disclaimer, Privacy and Security pages were
inspected. Its robots group does not exclude these printed public paths. No
relevant collection prohibition or blanket open-republication license was found.
The exported research contains short original factual paraphrases and source
links, not complete legal text, pamphlet prose, advocate arguments, images,
contacts or petition/sponsor/signature records. Access, factual reuse and public
editorial approval remain distinct.

## Factual mapping

- **Measure 1:** the [official ballot explanation](https://www.sos.nd.gov/sites/default/files/documents/elections/measures/ballot-language-2026-general-measure1.pdf)
  describes a 60% threshold for constitutional amendments placed before voters by
  legislative referral or initiative. The denominator is votes cast on the
  question, not all registered voters or a 60% legislative vote. The scanned
  HCR 3003 was visually reviewed, including its statement of intent and redlines.
  The official estimate says no fiscal impact but supplies no amount or horizon;
  missing numeric fields are not filled with invented annual zeros.
- **Measure 2:** the [official ballot explanation](https://www.sos.nd.gov/sites/default/files/documents/elections/measures/ballot-language-2026-general-measure2.pdf)
  concerns school meals. The proposed text distinguishes required public-school
  provision from optional nonpublic/Bureau of Indian Education/tribal provision,
  and excludes higher education. It specifies 2025-26 reimbursement rates with
  inflation adjustments and other-government reimbursement offsets, not each
  school's actual full meal cost. The Legacy Earnings Fund is a fallback when
  the legislature cannot identify another funding source.
- The [Legislative Council fiscal report](https://www.sos.nd.gov/sites/default/files/documents/elections/measures/schoollunches-fiscal-impact.pdf)
  attributes the 2027-29 projections to the Department of Public Instruction:
  $124-$134 million in meal costs plus $300,000 departmental administration. These
  reconcile to the ballot explanation's $124.3-$134.3 million additional ongoing
  biennial expenditure range. They are not annual costs, guaranteed amounts,
  household costs or savings. Administrative recurrence is not invented.
- The proposed text's school-year-relative implementation rule, at least 90 days
  after effectiveness, stays separate from the Council's 2027-28 projection. No
  exact effective date or calendar implementation date is inferred.
- One explicit review hold preserves a wording difference: the ballot explanation
  refers to conflicting constitutional provisions, while proposed section 7(11)
  refers to conflicting North Dakota law. No simplified unified legal rule is
  approved or silently substituted.

Two CSV/JSONL research records carry 18 field claims, document/page links, hashes,
capture times, attribution, structured fiscal ranges and the unresolved hold.
The four-member private packet excludes raw PDFs and evidence quotations. It is
research, not an application measure snapshot or approved public dossier. Only
the fiscal report's printed July 2026 month is recorded as a publication date;
capture times, URL directories and PDF metadata are not invented update dates.

## Site access and simplicity

North Dakota lookup results now offer **Statewide ballot measures**, linking
directly to the verified official index. Its existing election-office directory
remains available. A source-level `includeElectionOfficeDirectory` option lets
later state tool additions retain that fallback without duplicating directory
configuration. Georgia, Utah and other states' preceding resources are unchanged.

Only the necessary link label is added. There is no extra introduction, question
count, disclosure toggle, explanatory card, new navigation page or unapproved
measure summary. The current-index URL does not hard-code this year's
question count. Automatic PDF collection or editorial refresh is not enabled.
