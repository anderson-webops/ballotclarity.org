# New Mexico measures and source-access holds

## Scope

Seven November 3, 2026 statewide questions are prepared as an unpublished native
guide: four constitutional amendments and three general obligation bond
questions. Four Santa Fe County bond questions are exported separately as
county-scoped research. Preparation, collection, editorial approval, import and
publication remain distinct states.

The Legislative Council Service's
[2026 booklet](https://www.nmlegis.gov/Publications/New_Mexico_State_Government/Constitutional_Amendment/Constitutional_Amendments_2026.pdf)
supports the amendment explanations. PDF pages 10, 14, 22 and 27 were visually
reviewed. A retained, hash-verified
[Santa Fe County sample ballot](https://www.santafecountynm.gov/uploads/documents/Sample-SANT_PCT001.pdf)
confirms the election date, statewide questions, response labels and county
scope. Its two scanned pages were reviewed visually, not treated as an empty
text-only PDF.

## Content and scope

- Amendment 1 distinguishes the written-veto requirement and the twenty-day
  post-adjournment rule from an immediate approval of every pending bill.
- Amendment 2 concerns school-election timing, not automatically partisan school
  candidates or mandatory consolidation of every school election.
- Amendment 3 retains the distinction between nonstudent nominating committees
  and elected student-body nomination lists, including the twelve-month party
  registration qualification for nonstudent nominees.
- Amendment 4 uses the 2029 implementation date and income-linked compensation
  limits, not the booklet's illustrative 2026 income as a fixed future salary.
- State bond principal limits are $21,582,213, $19,324,188 and $352,225,379.
  Principal is not total repayment. The explanation preserves the property-tax
  levy, interest and issuance-expense implications without inventing a final
  interest rate or total debt service.
- The statewide choices are **For / Against**. County questions instead print
  **For general obligation bonds / Against general obligation bonds**. These
  labels are not replaced with generic Yes/No wording.

The county export covers roads ($14,035,000), water and wastewater ($6,500,000),
parks/trails/open space ($7,010,000) and fire facilities ($17,455,000). It is not
assigned statewide. A county-only guide would currently supersede, rather than
roll up, the statewide guide in location lookup. Its native integration is
therefore held until combined state/local presentation is supported. The
statewide guide continues to disclose its lack of local races and measures.

Only short factual paraphrases are prepared. No general open-license conclusion
is asserted for the booklet, and no advocate statements or staff-generated
argument lists are attributed to invented supporters or opponents. Unknown
publication dates remain unknown. Retained captures keep their original hashes
and retrieval times.

## Configurable collection

`nm-measure-documents-2026` pins one reviewed Legislative Council booklet for
the exact election cycle. Policy review binds the complete, unique `full-width`
policy region and observed policy/index links. Region identifiers and class
tokens are explicit and mutually exclusive; missing or duplicated regions fail
closed. Reviewed unrelated robots groups may precede the mandatory wildcard,
but cannot override its restrictions. Applicable, unknown, merged or repeated
groups still require review. Encoded document paths remain rejected.

Raw document collection uses external cache/output directories and never imports
or publishes content. Future election cycles and changed policies or document
sets require explicit configuration and review. The county ballot is a retained
supplement, not a newly automated county crawler.

## Access holds and correction

An existing Nebraska linked-use-policy hold was rediscovered after two premature
Nebraska PDF downloads in this cohort. The initial review missed a policy linked
from the publisher homepage, although the elections index itself had no such
link. Those captures and derived drafts are preserved in local quarantine, not
accepted for transfer, import or publication. The newly added Nebraska document
collector entry is removed. The prior 569-row candidate snapshot and earlier
hold evidence remain unchanged and are not added to accepted candidate inputs.
The pamphlet's printed reproduction permission does not silently resolve the
linked policy's scope or the separately published legislative statement.

Ohio and Nevada robots requests returned 403. Montana's guide index returned
403 after its robots check. South Dakota's linked policies redirect to
authentication, leaving its pamphlet review incomplete. No alternative identity,
host, mirror or archive is used to bypass these conditions. Independent existing
sources are neither deleted nor newly approved by this review.

## Verification

Local verification passes: 1,006 unit tests, with two optional PostgreSQL skips;
lint and typecheck; both builds and 104 compiled runtime modules; 21 built-app
integration checks; 48 core accessibility cases; and 76 New Mexico connected
desktop/mobile light/dark rendering and accessibility cases. Four keyboard,
choice-saving, reload and print journeys preserve the printed For/Against
labels, source-page links and partial-coverage disclosure. Three current page
screenshots and six source-page images were visually reviewed.

Twenty-four preparation outputs match between source and compiled execution.
The primary guide, fifteen previous additional guides, fifty candidate captures,
25,031 candidate rows and their original capture dates remain unchanged. Source
and compiled document-collector replays make zero publisher requests. County
research remains unavailable through native measure routes, and the two
independent draft/published test configurations do not grant actual publication
approval. Existing static-content UI remains unchanged.

Preserved diagnostics distinguish sandbox loopback denial and two test-harness
issues from application defects: held Nebraska data is intentionally absent
from accepted candidate inputs, and saved labels use faithful For/Against
casing rather than the copied harness's uppercase expectation. The passing
checks retain the original assertions and do not accept held source records.

No production activation, source publication, private data transfer, active
import or scheduler installation is part of this local cohort.
