# Reviewed candidate PDF context

The document collector supports an explicitly reviewed single PDF without
inventing an HTML discovery page. Its exact HTTPS address and singleton bounds
are configuration-bound. A current `indexPdf` fingerprint pins the original
bytes. Existing publisher policy, crawler restrictions, spacing, expiry,
conditional requests, durable holds and last-good retention still apply.
HTML collectors retain their separate link and content-marker checks.

Official candidate context can declare `pdfMaximumPages` from 1 to 25 on a
configured reference document. Preparation checks the original PDF hash and a
page-preserving extracted-text fingerprint. The existing bounded Poppler worker
is required only for preparation, not public/API reads. HTML region selectors
cannot be applied to PDFs. Changed content requires a renewed review before
new facts can be prepared. Claims remain explicitly reviewed rather than
generated from arbitrary PDF text.

The first source is South Fulton's May 10, 2022 District 7 vacancy notice.
One short historical service fact is associated with Mark Baker's exact retained
Fulton record. Its original publication and capture dates remain separate. This
does not establish a current office, incumbency, nomination, or eligibility, and
the existing 98-person roster remains authoritative for November candidate
status. The media contact and unrelated historical election logistics are omitted.

The city's published crawler groups are handled by the existing restrictive
reviewed layout. Its normally retrieved home page offered no additional legal
links. Original policy/footer fingerprints remain monitored. The reviewed PDF
is summarized, not republished as profile text.

Two separate county primary-filing URLs returned redirects to `/404`. Those
documents are unavailable, not evidence of an empty candidate field. Neither
alternate hosts nor archived copies were used to replace them. Restricted
campaign sources remain held.

Source preparation and validation do not authorize production import,
publication, scheduling, or protected deployment. A future election requires
its own actual roster association and current access/content review; annual
filenames and nominations are never guessed.
