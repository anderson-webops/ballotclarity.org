# Hamilton County, Tennessee: reusable ballot collection

The county's [sample-ballot index](https://elect.hamiltontn.gov/sampleballots.aspx)
offers a [five-page HTML general-election sample](https://elect.hamiltontn.gov/Portals/12/HTML/GenSample.html).
Normal public requests on October 27, 2026 retrieved the index, home page, robots
file, linked policy pages and publication. The publication is a sample/proofing set, not a verified
personal ballot or a certification of complete final eligibility.

## Imported facts

The publication contains 82 named candidates in 19 offices and six questions:
three Tennessee constitutional amendments, East Ridge Charter Amendment 1, a
Red Bank charter referendum and a Walden bond referendum. Thirty blank write-in
lines are voting spaces, not additional candidates. Forty-four printed party
labels are preserved; the 38 municipal candidates have no printed party label,
so none is inferred. Vote limits are explicit instructions, not invented seat
counts. The source's general list does not establish precinct applicability.

Question text preserves paragraph boundaries, jurisdiction, original choices
and source pages. `For` / `Against` and `For the amendment` / `Against the
amendment` remain distinct from `Yes` / `No`. Constitutional analytical
summaries are fingerprinted as evidence, not republished as question text.
No advocate statement, biography or source artwork is copied into application
records. No publication date is invented from Election Day.

## Automated contract

`tn-hamilton-2026` supplements the existing Williamson County source. It does
not replace its records or default priority. Future cycles follow the actual
offered public HTML link and require agreement between the publication title,
visible election header and accessible election heading. No filename, date,
candidate roster or primary-to-general nomination is predicted.

The reviewed stylesheet, semantic columns, candidate rows, blank write-ins,
question continuations, choices and page footers are reconciled together.
Unknown fields, hidden styling, changed policies, missing accepted candidates
or questions, incomplete publications, access failures and changed layouts
retain accepted data for review. The only recognized third-party beacon is
neither executed nor fetched. Collection needs no native PDF worker.

The robots response contains commented Content-Signal explanations and no
active restriction. The home/index links include a public-records request policy
and a public-meeting comment policy. These concern formal records requests and
meeting participation, not a blanket website-reuse prohibition. Both notices are
captured and fingerprinted before publication collection. Changed text or links
require renewed review. Login is never followed.

The raw publication SHA-256 is

The durable source digest additionally binds its offered URL. Public API reads
use stored facts without requesting the publisher. Source release, factual
transfer, receiving-host approval/scheduling and live acceptance are separate.

## Other source leads checked in this batch

- [New York City candidate index](https://www.vote.nyc/page/list-candidates):
  actual November 3, 2026 PDF and RTF links are public. The PDF has 44 pages.
  The RTF uses absolutely positioned drawing/text objects rather than tables.
  These require a separately reviewed bounded parser; no roster is imported
  merely because text can be extracted. Existing Monroe County data remains.
- [Douglas County, Nebraska](https://www.votedouglascounty-ne.gov/candidate_info.aspx):
  the site's actual public election-selector form returns a General Election
  roster through a normal form submission. Its linked calendar explicitly
  dates the election November 3, 2026. An actually offered candidate profile
  contains party and campaign-site fields absent from the roster. This is a
  promising independent county publisher, not clearance of the separate held
  Nebraska Secretary of State source. A reviewed collector and field-level
  reconciliation remain necessary. Denied robots paths are not requested.
- [Indianapolis sample ballots](https://vote.indy.gov/sample-ballot/):
  the page offers a GIS viewer, but its linked privacy policy requires permission
  before use/reproduction of site content. The viewer and underlying data were
  not collected. This remains a permission hold, not an invitation to use an
  undocumented endpoint.
