# Kanawha County recurring collection

## Scope

The county's [election index](https://kanawha.us/county-clerk/election-center/)
offers a public pre-election zero-test report. Its current thirteen-page report
contains 110 candidate/office records: three U.S. Senate, three U.S. House, nine
state Senate, eighteen House of Delegates and 77 local entries in sixty contests.
The [source preparation](2026-10-27-kanawha-preparation.md) retains the original
publication, date precision, access review and scope limitations.

`wv-kanawha-2026` is an external-data family, not a replacement for the 564
reviewed statewide records. County, municipal, ward and unexpired-term contexts
remain separate. District applicability remains withheld, ballot preview is
disabled and broader statewide sources retain their default priority. Listed
names do not establish qualification, nomination or personal voter eligibility.

## Future elections

Discovery associates visible offered report links with an unambiguous election
heading. The complete report supplies the actual election date; a year, URL or
calendar calculation alone cannot create a cycle. Future compatible
publications use `wv-kanawha-general-YYYY-MM-DD`. Synthetic future fixtures test
the mechanism without claiming a future publication has been observed.

Each collection rechecks robots, the home/index legal-link inventory and the
three linked county policies before requesting a report. Reviewed same-host
trailing-slash redirects are explicit and bounded. Policy text is checked by its
normalized article fingerprint; captures retain policy provenance digests, not
copies of unrelated legal prose. New links, changed policies, denied requests
and unknown redirects require review. The separate statewide host's access and
reuse boundaries remain unchanged.

A November 1 recheck found the same offered report URL under the county's
dated “Unofficial Results” link and matching general-election-results heading.
Discovery supports that explicit label and deduplicates identical test/results
aliases; inconsistent years or competing report URLs require review. The link
label never changes the native acceptance rule: nonzero voting/reporting totals
are not candidate-roster data and retain the previous accepted snapshot.

## Native and stored facts

Maintained QPDF and Poppler workers retain their input, output, page and time
bounds. The native guard accepts the reviewed standard Helvetica/WinAnsi
resources, explicit black text, identity transforms and bounded table drawing
instructions. Independent word boxes reconcile every native text field and the
complete layout. The guard accounts for reviewed one-point column borders and
half of the report's 0.01-point printed-coordinate resolution. Other overlays,
hidden text, changed fonts/mappings, clipped layouts, unfamiliar operators,
nonzero voting/reporting and incomplete pagination require review.

Imports preserve literal source parties, offices, jurisdiction, voting limits
and unexpired terms. The printed publication date remains distinct from
retrieval; its unzoned minute clock is not converted into an invented instant.
Zero counts, voter statistics, empty nomination slots and write-in totals are
not imported as people or results. Four question headings and choices remain
research inventories because the report does not contain their full wording.

The existing cycle catalog and external snapshot store provide repeatable
imports, restart recovery and last-known-good retention. Validated empty data
remains waiting rather than a successful zero-record import. Disappearing
reports, removed identities, older publications and persistence failures retain
accepted data for intervention. Public readers use persisted facts without
publisher access or native tools.

## Acceptance boundary

Local acceptance covers actual native collection, all 110 original records,
managed first/repeat imports, restart recovery, source metadata, complete public
API pagination, responsive rendering and accessibility. The existing 564
statewide records and 108 candidate websites are independent regression cases.
Raw publications stay outside Git and release assets. A source release and
local data packet are distinct from receiving-host import and public activation.
