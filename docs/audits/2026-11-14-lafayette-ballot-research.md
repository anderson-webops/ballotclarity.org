# Lafayette December ballot research

Reviewed November 14, 2026. [One Acadiana's election hub](https://www.oneacadiana.org/advocacy/2026-elections-hub/)
offers a six-page [December 12 ballot guide](https://www.oneacadiana.org/wp-content/uploads/GeauxVote_Dec2026_BallotGuide-FINAL-v2.pdf).
All six original pages were rendered and reviewed. The publisher's empty HTTP
200 robots file and linked privacy policy supplied no additional automated-access
restriction. Its copyright notice remains operative; research retains factual
fields and citations, with explanations/artwork excluded from public reuse.

The [government announcement](https://www.lafayettela.gov/news/posts/lcg-launches-info-hub-ahead-of-december-12-millage-renewals/)
confirms five parishwide and four city propositions. Its offered `/millage` URL
redirects to `lafayettela.info`, whose canonical `www` page identifies Lafayette
Consolidated Government. Original redirect and policy evidence are retained.
The government robots file permits these paths; the microsite has no robots file
(HTTP 404). The microsite's claims and advocacy language are not neutral findings.

All nine question texts reconcile between the guide and the
[government information page](https://www.lafayettela.info/) after Unicode,
whitespace and quotation normalization. Five question numbers conflict:

| Printed area and subject | Guide number | Government page number |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Parish library | 2 | 5 |
| Parish courthouse/jail | 5 | 2 |
| City recreation | 1 | 4 |
| City public buildings | 2 | 1 |
| City public safety | 4 | 2 |

The information page also labels its council resolutions `PR-XXX-2026` and
`CR-XXX-2026`. The council archive independently identifies the adopted calls as
`PR-010-2026` and `CR-015-2026`. Its complete August 4 meeting search returned
43 documents without truncation, including both resolutions and both councils'
minutes. Document IDs came from those public search results.

The [certified parish resolution](https://apps.lafayettela.gov/obcouncil/api/Document/2574677/)
contains five propositions on PDF pages 1-2 and repeats them in Exhibit A on
pages 7-8. The adoption/signature is on page 5 and the clerk's signed certification
on page 12. The [certified city resolution](https://apps.lafayettela.gov/obcouncil/api/Document/2574696/)
contains four propositions on pages 1-2 and repeats them in Exhibit A on page 5;
adoption, signature and certification appear on pages 3, 4 and 8 respectively.
All twenty scanned pages were rendered and visually reviewed. Native extraction
returns no question text; imperfect OCR remains a review aid.

Both calls name December 12, 2026. All nine proposition numbers, subjects, question
bodies and fiscal amounts in the calls agree with the One Acadiana guide.
The five microsite numbering conflicts therefore have independent evidence
supporting the guide's numbering **in the adopted council calls**. Both
[parish minutes](https://apps.lafayettela.gov/obcouncil/api/Document/2577980/)
and [city minutes](https://apps.lafayettela.gov/obcouncil/api/Document/2577984/)
corroborate adoption on their PDF page 2.

The resolutions make the election call subject to State Bond Commission approval
and allow regulatory amendments before notice publication. The certified copies
establish the adopted calls, while final sample-ballot numbering, subsequent
amendments and personal applicability remain separate checks. The original
preparer output and its unset canonical ballot numbers remain unchanged;
versioned research records the resolution numbers and original publisher conflict.

The nine factual rows preserve the separate parish/city areas, eight continuation
rates and estimated annual revenues, printed tax years, and the bond's distinct
$77 million maximum authorization, thirty-year maximum term and seven-percent
interest ceiling. These figures do not guarantee borrowing terms, collections,
personal tax liability or election outcomes. The guide's two City Court names
are source-stated runoff references; no newly certified candidate roster is
inferred.

The [maintained offline preparer](../lafayette-ballot-research.md) exports pinned
JSON/CSV research and holds ambiguous numbers automatically. The independent
council evidence supplies reviewable resolution-level corroboration. Current
publication, rights review, final sample-ballot reconciliation, geographic
applicability, import and recurring acquisition remain separate. The state-portal
access hold is unchanged. The first parish download returned HTTP 500; one bounded
retry later returned the complete twelve-page PDF. Both outcomes are retained.
