# Town of Dane candidate automation

## Source and reconciliation

The town's [news index](https://townofdane.gov/news-notices/) offers a dated
[general-election article](https://townofdane.gov/2026/09/16/november-3-2026-general-election-information/).
That article links the Lodi, Waunakee and Sauk school-area sample ballots and a
separate county Type B notice. Collection uses those offered controls rather than
guessing an election year, date, article or document URL.

Native extraction reproduces all fifteen reviewed candidate/ticket fields and IDs.
Each sample has seventeen named people represented by fifteen choices: two joint
governor/lieutenant-governor tickets. There are nine statewide records, one
congressional, one Senate, two Assembly and two county records. Literal parties
are five Republican, nine Democratic and one Wisconsin Green. The notice supplies
three independent legislative claims, not three extra candidates. No single-name
office is inferred unopposed or elected.

The native PDF groups one congressional vote-limit line with instructions from
the adjacent column. Word coordinates, not the merged line's left edge, separate
those columns. All line/word bounds, dates, office/party boundaries and ticket rows
are validated. The Lodi and Waunakee reverse sides have school questions and numbered
two-sided footers; Sauk instead has its actual administration-only reverse side.
New candidate controls in the referendum column or reverse side require review.
All eight pages remain required. Household contacts, polling signatures and the
notice's signing clerk are not candidate records. School questions remain separate
research; overlapping ward labels establish no personal school eligibility.

## Access and reuse

Current normal requests verify the town, document-host and canonical vendor robots
files, the complete town legal/footer inventory and the linked vendor
[privacy policy](https://townweb.com/privacy-policy/) before PDFs are requested.
The town's separate Amazonbot group is explicitly reviewed through the maintained
named-group interface; it is not an AI identity override. The town's event-path
denials remain enforced. The independent restricted Sauk County website and other
Wisconsin access holds are unchanged.

The vendor's published www URLs have two observed canonical 301 responses: robots
and privacy, each to its exact same-path non-www destination. The public document
helper exposes bounded redirect metadata but still follows no redirects. This
family accepts only those two exact reviewed responses; every destination receives
its own pinned public request and applicable policy checks. Unexpected targets,
statuses, policies and private destinations remain refusal conditions.

The publisher varies email-protection fragments between otherwise identical policy
responses, including a long RFP contact link. A source-local fingerprint normalizes
only bounded hexadecimal fragments on the exact relative email-protection path.
Visible policy text, legal-link destinations and footer content remain hashed;
new terms and substantive changes still stop collection. No email is decoded or
contact endpoint requested. The underlying generic policy checker is unchanged.

No applicable prohibition on extracting factual candidate fields was found in the
reviewed linked privacy policy. This is not a blanket republication license for
PDF design, photographs, prose or other third-party material. Rhode Island's
independent East Greenwich robots file requires supported named-AI-group review;
no new Rhode Island index or candidate document is fetched. This is not a claim
that all AI or election-path access there is prohibited.

## Reusable and recovery contract

The current source identity is retained. Bounded, actually offered news pagination,
dated article headings and distinct sample/notice roles drive discovery. Compatible
future dates require no annual source edit; synthetic future fixtures establish
schema compatibility, not the existence of future ballots. Changed office layouts
and newly offered styles require review instead of inferred records.

All publications must be present before import. Shared statewide/county claims and
the complete notice's legislative list reconcile independently. Changed same-URL
hashes invalidate cached imports. Disappearing accepted identities or publications
retain accepted data for review. The first automatic replacement preserves the
exact manual envelope, and a failed recovery backup preserves the accepted target.
The bounded Poppler worker is needed for collection, not persisted reads.

The state and local directories remain source-specific rather than assertions of
complete Wisconsin coverage or a personalized ballot. Existing source approval,
schedule, data receipt, import and activation gates retain separate evidence.
