# Juneau municipal candidate collection

The independently administered [Juneau municipal election index](https://juneau.org/clerk/elections)
offers a [certified candidate roster](https://juneau.org/clerk/certified-municipal-candidates-2026)
for October 6, 2026. Eleven candidates occupy four office groups: Assembly
Areawide, Assembly District 1, Assembly District 2 and Board of Education.
The publisher explicitly describes every race as elected at-large. Assembly
district numbers describe candidate residency, rather than voter eligibility.
The three-year terms and printed seat counts are retained. Party, incumbency,
write-in completeness, election results and current officeholding remain unknown
in this candidate dataset. The newer November state-election notice is separate.
