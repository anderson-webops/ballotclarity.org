# Fulton background sources

Two independently reviewed collections add five short background facts for
Bridget Thorne and Lynn King. Thorne's government biography supplies professional
and educational background; King's campaign supplies her own professional and
organizational history. Each retains its actual publication kind and publisher.
Party, incumbency, ballot qualification, office sought and geographic applicability
remain controlled by the original official candidate records.

The reusable reviewed-context preparer now has an explicitly opted-in official
biography mode. It shares the existing size, exact-identity, source/election,
capture-hash, meaningful-content, output, expiry and collection safeguards.
Candidate-statement commands, review types and durable hold names remain unchanged.
A preparation label cannot turn campaign material into government attribution.
Official biography bindings require a configured collection's explicit capability;
other biography datasets retain their previous refusal.

Thorne's biography is an exact, unique `title-text` region on a profile offered
by the county's commissioner roster. Updated menus or tracking scripts do not
silently become biography facts. Meaningful biography changes require renewed
review and preserve the accepted snapshot. King's normally linked About page
uses its main region and remains candidate-provided. Neither collection imports
unreviewed photographs, contacts, family details, endorsements or campaign appeals.

South Fulton's city robots publication contains crawler-specific restrictions
requiring a separate layout/access review. Its index was not acquired. Mark
Baker's normally acquired campaign page is a client-rendered shell without a
native biography region; this is not a finding that he lacks a biography.
Earlier campaign restrictions remain separate. An unpublished-looking alternative
county host was not used for Mo Ivory's biography. These research holds do not
determine anyone's ballot status.
