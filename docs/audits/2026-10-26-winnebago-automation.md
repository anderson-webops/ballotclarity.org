# Winnebago County collection and reuse

## Source and access

The independently published [county election index](https://wincoil.gov/departments/clerks-office/election-information)
offered the November 3, 2026 general-election candidate list and write-in
supplement. The ordinary certificate-verified, public-IP-pinned collector returned
HTTP 200 for robots, the index, its linked accessibility statement and both PDFs.
The reviewed county policies permit these requests; no alternate identity,
challenge bypass or Illinois State Board of Elections source was used.

The candidate PDF SHA-256 remains
`8c4af8e59fe422ed305c56b0c9a73035deb0122b2e2e6b7bb927b27886e58167`;
the write-in supplement remains
`2bba9efff38d871ecc30c39ff87aca8ab7698ad7aad7156400500c4e8cf57383`.

## Facts and fidelity

The five regular-list pages contribute 58 records, preserving every previously
reviewed field and identity. The write-in page prints fourteen people: two
explicit governor/lieutenant-governor tickets and four nonexecutive entries yield
six additional records. Six unpaired executive entries remain excluded. Party is
not inferred for write-ins. The final inventory is 64 records, not a complete
personalized ballot: 16 statewide, five congressional, four state-senate, seven
state-house and 32 local/regional records.

Separate named judicial-retention questions, vacancy labels, the unexpired
county-board term, independent-sheriff override and nonpartisan school offices
remain distinct. Native text and original PDF objects are checked against visible
SVG glyphs on all six pages. Standard font encodings and the exact reviewed
dash-only embedded font are bounded. Candidate glyph clipping is accepted only
when the full calculated ink bounds fit the actual rectangular clip. Hidden or
white text, missing glyphs, opaque overlays, unsupported graphics, changed font
maps and active PDF features require review rather than silent extraction.

## Reusable collection

The `il-winnebago-2026` family discovers dates and publication URLs from actual
offered controls. Compatible later general elections receive dated source IDs;
unpublished documents stay awaiting records. Tests of a synthetic 2028 election
prove pending-to-available behavior, not an actual future publication. Primary
and consolidated elections are not extrapolated into general-election data.

The explicit collection worker uses bounded maintained Poppler and qpdf tools.
Accepted envelopes and normalized snapshots are durable external state. Public
reads do not execute converters or contact the county, and see newly stored
imports without restarting. Repeated unchanged imports retain capture clocks;
same-URL PDF hash revisions invalidate the import cache. Disappearing accepted
publication kinds, lost candidate identities and corrupt proofs preserve the
accepted catalog/data for review. County-only lookup never assigns local seats.

Local actual-data acceptance covers all 64 records, the exact original 58,
six rendered/native proofs, durable import/cache behavior, revision handling,
pending transitions, corruption rejection and disappearance recovery. Published
source, host artifact acceptance, scheduled collection and data receipt remain
separate outcomes.
