# Civic participation resources

The public `/get-involved` entry links to meeting, service, and candidacy guides.
National government directories remain separate from Fulton County, Georgia
party and government procedures. Party resources appear alphabetically with
the same presentation. The pages collect no applications or party preferences.

## Source review

Primary publisher resources were checked November 6, 2026. The typed catalog in
`front-end/src/data/civic-participation.ts` records the publisher, URL, review
time, jurisdiction grouping, role, steps, and links. These resources are shipped
with source; adding a jurisdiction means adding reviewed content to that catalog.

- [Fulton Democrats](https://fultondems.org/our-party/) distinguish general
  membership from voting post seats selected by caucus or vacancy procedures.
- [Fulton Republicans](https://fultongagop.org/conventions) distinguish precinct
  officers, delegates, and convention stages. Their explanatory page retains
  2025 dates, so the guide directs readers to a current meeting call.
- [Georgia Libertarians](https://lpgeorgia.com/about/affiliates/fulton/) identify
  the county affiliate. The current calendar and event detail now both list
  7 p.m. on November 24; the earlier researched timing conflict is resolved in
  the reviewed pages. The guide links to the current calendar rather than
  publishing a meeting instance or inferring its time zone.
- [Fulton County](https://www.fultoncountyga.gov/commissioners) provides comment
  rules, agendas, schedules, boards, and election-worker resources.
- [Georgia candidate guidance](https://sos.ga.gov/candidate-qualifying-elected-office)
  separates qualifying from campaign-finance obligations. Its 2026 general
  qualifying window is historical, not a future deadline.
- [USAGov](https://www.usa.gov/local-governments), the
  [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/help-america-vote), and the
  [FEC](https://www.fec.gov/help-candidates-and-committees/registering-candidate/)
  supply national starting points.

## Maintenance boundary

The guide publishes process guidance and direct publisher links, not a live
meeting or vacancy feed. Recurrences, old dates, and directory membership do not
establish a future meeting, selection deadline, or open seat. Future event
collection must retain source evidence, distinguish selection from social events,
and hold conflicting, expired, or time-zone-uncertain instances for review.
Party participation follows each organization's local rules independently of
public-election cycles. No party affinity is inferred from a location.
