# Clark County historical layout compatibility

The [official 2024 election index](https://www.clarkcountynv.gov/government/departments/elections/24g-info)
offers the fourteen-page [general-election candidate table](https://www.clarkcountynv.gov/adobe/assets/urn:aaid:aem:64d1b48e-07cd-432d-90ca-32c470ac8985/original/as/officesup-24g.pdf).
Its SHA-256 is `3d16fccadea89baf33d51db1c9bbd57f8ae63ebd9778dae46af60cfde511e32f`.
The source has 195 candidate entries, including four joint presidential tickets,
and five separate nonperson options. These are historical facts, not current or
future candidates. Ninety-four entries concern local or regional offices.

The table reader accepts this observed landscape layout alongside the current
portrait layout. Presidential first-name and surname columns pair within each
printed ticket. Printed Senate, education, regent, power-district, advisory-board
and township labels normalize without inventing missing departments or personal
ballot eligibility. Fourteen entries retain an explicit five-vote limit.

The landscape publication's three-point opaque black border previously merged
two judicial rows during extraction. Bounded filled-rule recognition now retains
that actual border. The separate stroked-ballot reader is unchanged. Candidate
tables also require the complete printed page count and sequence; ambiguous
names, unknown offices and changed geometry remain review exceptions.

## Evidence and automation boundary

Robots, linked policy and actually offered publication links were rechecked on
October 25. The original candidate PDF was retained with its original capture
time, not described as a fresh download. Factual table and selected text/vector
regressions exclude document graphics and unrelated personal data.

The [Fall 2024 newsletter](https://www.clarkcountynv.gov/adobe/assets/urn:aaid:aem:094026b5-5399-4b7c-adcb-e550107c53b5/original/as/cced-newsletter-oct-nov-dec-2024.pdf)
contains no candidate roster. It cannot satisfy the existing independent
publication requirement. This change neither enables a historical import nor
relaxes that gate. Synthetic future presidential tests establish parser and
workflow compatibility only; different publications and layouts still require
review. Current 2026 collection retains both independently reconciled rosters,
all 254 accepted candidate identities and fields, and eight nonperson options.
Canonical data and receiving-host state remain separate from source release.
