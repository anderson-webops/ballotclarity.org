# Louisiana local background and official-source review

## Scope and attribution

The v1.11.2 collection adds nine local profiles and 21 background facts from
original Webster Parish Journal candidate announcements. Six concern school-board
Districts 3/7/9/11, two concern Minden City Council District C, and one concerns
Springhill mayor. All 58 preceding configurations and 284 profiles remain exact.
The new totals are 59 collections, 293 profiles and 660 facts across 50 states
plus DC. This does not complete candidate, challenger, local or ballot coverage.

These are candidate-provided submissions, not independent reporting or official
qualification. Third-person presentation/editor bylines do not change authorship.
Only brief factual paraphrases of education, professional history, military/public
service and volunteering are included. Campaign appeals, generic priorities,
family/religion, contacts, photos and ballot numbers are omitted. Janice
Lindsey-Keener's announcement supplied no substantive background fact, so it was
not padded into a profile. Search-only Jeanine Franklin/Stewart/Evans leads remain
unaccepted; cached author-page positions did not establish current source bodies.
Myles/Pendergrass filing leads are not imported as active candidates. A missing
search result is not proof against withdrawal, disqualification or later changes.

McMurray's additional educational-leadership credit hours are not a degree.
Melancon's LSU Shreveport studies do not establish graduation. Hardy's military
service is stated without invented rank, deployment or licensing claims; an
ambiguous degree sentence is omitted. Date-stamped statements of employment and
service are attributed background, not an independently verified current role.
Several WordPress posts were modified before their scheduled publication date;
the original publication metadata and displayed date agree and remain distinct
from capture/modification. No filing date is inferred from an announcement date.
