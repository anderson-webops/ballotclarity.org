# East Greenwich native-reader preparation

## Verified source

The [town election index](https://www.eastgreenwichri.gov/192/Voter-Registration-Elections)
offers five precinct samples for November 3, 2026. Its current dated heading and
links agree with the existing reviewed source. Each two-page sample contributes
32 candidate records; their union is 33 records because precinct 0903 prints
House District 24 while the other four print House District 30. No precinct-to-voter
assignment is inferred.

Current normal pinned requests match all five original PDF hashes. All ten pages
remain required, and the native extraction accounts for 160 repeated candidate
claims without manufacturing newer capture clocks for the active manual import.

The native reader reproduces all existing fields and identifiers, including
`Micholas A. Credle`, literal party labels, terms, vote limits and thirteen local
records. Repeated claims agree across publications. A one-name office remains
listed rather than becoming an inferred unopposed result. The reverse sides
contain questions, not additional candidate records; no measure publication is
introduced by this preparation.

## Policy review

The current [robots file](https://www.eastgreenwichri.gov/robots.txt) has wildcard
restrictions and a separate multi-agent AI/search group. That group disallows
calendar, alerts, search, administrative and other paths, not the offered election
index or DocumentCenter samples. The source-specific policy reader enforces both
sets of restrictions, uses the existing request identity and requires the exact
reviewed policy content. Line-ending normalization changes no directives.
The generic collector still refuses unreviewed named groups.

The complete index and linked [accessibility](https://www.eastgreenwichri.gov/accessibility),
[copyright](https://www.eastgreenwichri.gov/site/copyright) and
[privacy](https://www.eastgreenwichri.gov/privacy) content are captured before PDFs.
The copyright notice reserves publisher rights; it grants no blanket permission
to republish designs, photographs, signatures or prose. Preparation retains factual
candidate fields and provenance. No identity substitution, denied endpoint,
automatic redirect or access-control bypass is used. Rhode Island state-host and
other publisher holds remain independent.

An initial accessibility request timed out. One bounded request with the same
identity succeeded. A captured page's semantic analysis then required its actual
`mainWrapTS` region rather than a nonexistent HTML `main` element. The retained
body was analyzed without another request. The index's unrelated `//` placeholder
is recorded rather than treated as an election URL.

## Reader contract and remaining integration

The reader checks both 648-by-1260 pages, bounded line/word geometry, the four
specific watermark fragments, election/precinct headings, matching printed
districts, office and party boundaries, term/vote controls and the continuation
marker. Unknown or repeated offices, new parties, malformed rows, unrecognized
large text, missing pages and reverse-side candidate controls require review.
Synthetic 2030 fixtures verify compatible date/layout handling, not a future
publication or roster.

This is source preparation only. The active registry, reviewed import format,
current records, capture clocks and data packet remain unchanged. Automatic
discovery, catalog wiring, incomplete-publication handling, durable automatic
replacement and recovery still need integration before enabling this family.
The existing manual source remains the supported runtime path. There are still
37 reusable families across 29 states and D.C.; this preparation adds none to that
count and does not establish complete Rhode Island coverage.
