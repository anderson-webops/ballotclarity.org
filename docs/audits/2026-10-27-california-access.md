# California collection access review

The existing 388 reviewed California candidate records remain unchanged.

The Secretary of State's primary robots document was accessible, but
`https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/robots.txt` returned HTTP 403. The statewide
certification PDF was not collected through that denied host. San Francisco's
candidate page presented a browser challenge, which was not solved.

Independent San Diego and Orange County election indexes were accessible.
San Diego's offered general-election candidate PDF and Orange County's
qualified-contest inventory are research leads, not configured automatic
imports or evidence of complete statewide coverage. No new California factual
packet or automatically approved source is claimed by this review.

The independent Pima County source was developed instead while these access and
format limits remain explicit. California's existing records and provenance
are preserved pending a lawful, verified collection contract.
