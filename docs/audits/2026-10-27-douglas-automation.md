# Douglas County, Nebraska: reusable public filing records

The county's [candidate index](https://www.votedouglascounty-ne.gov/candidate_info.aspx)
offers a General Election selection through its ordinary public form. Its
[calendar](https://www.votedouglascounty-ne.gov/deadlines.aspx) explicitly dates
the election November 3, 2026. On October 27, 177 normal public responses
reconciled the policy/index/calendar, selected roster and 171 actually linked
candidate profiles. Office-detail and PDF paths prohibited by robots were not
requested. No voter lookup, account input, private endpoint or fabricated
postback event was used.

## Facts and interpretation

The selected roster has 80 office headings, 171 ordinary filings and two named
write-in filings. The names, filing dates and office/profile identifiers remain
bound together. The source establishes filings, not a complete qualified ballot.
Separate governor and lieutenant-governor filings remain separate; they are not
combined into invented tickets. Court entries remain filings, not constructed
ordinary races or retention voting choices. Neither precinct eligibility nor
the number of seats is inferred.

The profiles contain 169 personal party affiliations. Sixty-two labels are
retained as candidate fields: explicit affiliations for partisan offices, plus
explicit nonpartisan labels. The other 107 affiliations are retained as evidence
but not presented as a party nomination in a nonpartisan office. Blank fields
remain unknown. The site links to the actual public profiles. Candidate-provided
biographies, personal addresses, phone numbers, email addresses and longer
statements are not copied into application records.

## Automation

`ne-douglas-2026` follows actual general-election options, explicit calendar dates
and actually offered profile links. Synthetic 2028 and 2032 tests exercise the
same contract without annual URL edits. These tests do not establish that future
publications or people exist. The source is explicitly filing-only and cannot
produce a ballot preview, including when a caller omits its configured safeguard.
Existing broader ballot sources remain eligible independently.

Normal requests preserve the public form's hidden fields without alteration.
All-office selection, office inventories, profile identifiers, names, election
labels and filing dates must agree. Collection is bounded and spaced. Changed
policies, unknown filters/layouts, mismatched or missing profiles, denied requests
and disappearing accepted filings retain the prior data for review. Durable
imports are hash-bound; public readers do not contact the publisher. No native
PDF converter is needed.

The county's robots file permits the index, calendar, privacy page and candidate
profiles while excluding other paths. Its linked privacy policy describes
visitor-information handling and public access, with no identified blanket
automated-reuse prohibition. This is not an affirmative license for copying
biographies or artwork. Reviewed policy text and legal-link inventories are
fingerprinted; changed rules require renewed review. Copyright-year changes
alone do not imply changed terms.

This is an independent county publisher. The separate Nebraska Secretary of
State automated-use permission hold, existing reviewed 569-record snapshot and
independent reference collections remain unchanged. Earlier state captures are
not used to bypass that hold. Source publication, factual transfer, receiving-host
approval/scheduling and public acceptance remain separate.
