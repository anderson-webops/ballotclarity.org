# Fulton filing context

The county's current Candidate Information index links two original qualification
lists covering seven people already present in the retained November candidate
snapshot. Their printed dates are March 7 and May 2, 2026. The soil-and-water URL
contains a June date, but that is not its printed publication date.

The new optional collection extracts only reported occupations. Original names,
offices, parties, ballot qualification, incumbency, jurisdiction and eligibility
remain unchanged. In particular, the District 4 document's historical party and
incumbency columns do not overwrite the ballot. Two punctuation differences have
explicit reviewed name associations, rather than fuzzy matching.

Each reference retains its original PDF hash, capture date, printed publication
date and exact internal official record binding. Public responses omit the
internal binding. The public-dataset binding capability is opt-in for this
reviewed collection; other datasets retain their existing rejection behavior.

The existing bounded document collector supplies normal HTTPS, unchanged robots
and privacy fingerprints, offered-link checks, publisher spacing, daily caching,
review expiry and last-good retention. Native Poppler extraction runs in the
collection worker, not on public requests. A changed table, missing person,
ambiguous office or changed publication date requires review and retains accepted
content. A durable preparation hold stops repeat publisher requests.

The parser accepts an explicit general-election date for later cycles. Current
registry identities and reviewed URLs remain tied to 2026. Future-cycle enrollment
requires the newly offered documents and exact candidate associations, not inferred
nominees or constructed publication URLs. Preparation, import, publication and
recurring activation remain separate.

Primary index: [Fulton Candidate Information](https://fultoncountyga.gov/Inside-Fulton-County/Fulton-County-Departments/Registration-and-Elections/Candidate-Information).
