## Reusable contract

its exact manual descriptor remains available for reviewed preparation/enrichment
and recovery. Actually offered general-election headings, calendar dates and notice
links drive the reusable family. No guessed PDF URLs, primary-result advancement,
FEC inference or generated future candidate roster is used. Calendar and notice
dates must agree. Missing notices remain awaiting records; unsupported controls,
policy changes, disappearing accepted publications or identities require review.

All PDF pages have bounded geometry and an exact numbered/date-bearing footer.
The three candidate columns are read through their actual section boundaries,
including the final bottom row. Office groups, party suffixes, supported wrapped
rows, question boundaries and the printed unopposed marker are validated. Unknown
headings cannot silently become part of a candidate name. Named municipalities,
wards, townships and legislative districts remain outside unverified personal
matching. The county selector requires a unique non-IP county context; statewide
and generic Arkansas behavior retain the independent Washington source.

The policy-bound collection worker stores its data outside Git and runtime
artifacts. Same-URL document hashes invalidate imports when appropriate; unchanged
imports retain their original capture clocks. The first automatic replacement
retains the exact reviewed envelope for older-runtime data recovery, and a failed
backup keeps accepted data intact. Poppler is a collection-worker capability, not
a requirement for persisted reads. Future examples in tests are synthetic only.
