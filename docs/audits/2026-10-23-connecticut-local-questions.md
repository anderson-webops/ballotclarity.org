## Preparation and limits

The generic offline preparer accepts 19 exact municipal inputs, with source-page
citations and short original summaries. Each draft retains unknown publication
dates, partial coverage, explicit unresolved fields and false publication/import
approval. All 34 prior additional guides and the primary guide remain intact.
The combined review snapshot contains 53 additional guides, not an active import.

The explanations preserve consequential differences rather than repairing or
guessing the ballot text. Enfield's $511 million appropriation differs from its
$89 million borrowing cap, and unspecified grants are not treated as awarded.
Cheshire's borrowing authorizations finance the appropriations, not additional
costs. Plainfield's grant-match limit changes from $10,000 to $35,000. Danbury's
Board of Education terms are not assumed to match other elected offices.
Unspecified charter details, Coventry's missing percentage and Winchester's
unprinted budget deadlines remain identified limitations. West Haven's third
English and Spanish descriptions differ in detail; the summary follows the
English question without combining them into new claims.

Exact reviewed bilingual vote pairs remain intact: `Yes/Sí` / `No/No`,
`Yes/Sí` / `No`, and `Yes - Si` / `No - No`. Unreviewed translations, reversed
choices and mixed pairs remain rejected. These labels use the existing binary
decision keys and do not introduce a new vote type.

Burlington and Harwinton each print the same four Regional School District
Number 10 questions: $5 million for HVAC, $14.935 million for roofs, $3.9 million
for a synthetic-turf field, and $3.1 million for a natural-grass field. They are
four distinct district questions, not eight municipal questions. They remain
separately recorded and held because the municipal preparation contract does not
represent regional-school-district eligibility. Neither host town is substituted
for the district.

## Configurable raw collection

`ct-question-documents-2026` adds a reviewed raw-document configuration. It pins
21 complete public URLs, including their 32-hex `rev` and `hash` parameters.
Removing, replacing, duplicating or encoding those parameters is not accepted.
An explicit second-wildcard-group option merges both actual robots groups;
neither group's restrictions are discarded. The independently listed
`*semrush*` group is the only newly supported wildcard-bearing named token.
Unknown groups, additional duplicate groups and named AI/importer groups still
require review. The preceding nine source configurations are unchanged.

Current robots, homepage policy links, privacy policy, disclaimer and the linked
accessibility policy were reviewed through ordinary successful responses. The
accessibility PDF concerns state-agency accessibility responsibilities, not a
general reuse license. No broad reuse permission or legal determination is
inferred. State seals, logos, publisher artwork and candidate prose are not
included in the data packet. Source notices and applicable rights still govern.

The collector fingerprints the reviewed policies and index links, enforces
publisher spacing, bounds sizes, preserves last-good bytes and halts on access,
transport, policy, election or URL changes. Its source and compiled replays seed
26 retained captures and expose 21 unreviewed documents with zero publisher
requests. Raw collection never parses, imports or publishes a guide. Later
elections, changed versions and regional eligibility require their own review.

## Verification boundary

Local source/compiled preparation, exact lookup scope, publication gates, rendered
pages, saved decisions and printing are checked against the retained data.
Controlled test geography is not personal voter-eligibility evidence. Review
metadata remains unknown and workflows remain draft. No source release, active
import, data transfer, server scheduling or production administration is part of
this work.

The accepted local run records 1,076 unit passes with two optional PostgreSQL
skips, lint, typecheck, both builds, 21 integrated smoke tests and 48 core
accessibility cases. Fourteen preparation files match between source and compiled
commands. The actual-data review covers 484 render/accessibility cases, 76
keyboard/saved-choice/actual-print journeys and 46 positive/negative publication
and geography checks. Forty-nine retained candidate captures preserve all 24,891
rows. Three representative current screenshots were visually reviewed.

An initial long browser run reached the existing 60-request lookup limit. The
accepted run uses a fresh isolated API for each viewport, preserving that default
limit rather than disabling or raising it. An initial fixture type annotation and
an order-only collector assertion were corrected; their diagnostics are retained
separately and are not counted as successful runs.
