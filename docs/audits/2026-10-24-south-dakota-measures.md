  the source distinction: the government explanation describes ending expanded
  coverage below 90%, while the supporting argument describes ending the mandate.
  The complete operative amendment is required to resolve practical/legal effect;
  individual coverage and fiscal outcomes are not inferred.
- J: Explicit noncitizen voting disqualification, with citizenship already a
  qualification in the existing constitution. The final-linked document's letter
  calls its enclosure final, while the enclosure retains a DRAFT heading. The
  pamphlet independently prints the government explanation; its advocate column
  misnames Amendment G. Both source caveats remain in review data, not silently
  corrected or adopted as new voting-rights claims.

  general fund and distributions determined by the Legislature. Advocate return
  figures, guaranteed revenues and owner-right assurances are excluded. No
  submitted opposing statement does not establish that no opposition exists.

  retains the votes-cast denominator and does not expand the rule to every
