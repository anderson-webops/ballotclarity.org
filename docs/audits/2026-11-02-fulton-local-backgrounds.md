# Fulton local candidate backgrounds

Reviewed November 2, 2026. The retained official Fulton snapshot lists 17 local
candidates across county commission and soil/water offices. None had an attached
reference background in the preceding retained-data integration review.

## Exact local association

The official snapshot omits a separate jurisdiction label for those local rows.
The background preparer previously discarded the official source and record IDs
used in its review. Matching then required a jurisdiction label that neither
record contained, so otherwise reviewed local facts could not attach.

New local preparations retain an internal `officialCandidate` binding to the
validated official source and record ID. Both state/election context and exact
name, office, scope and district must still match. Conflicting known jurisdictions,
ambiguous official identities and mismatched bindings remain held. Missing
jurisdiction labels remain absent; no county, school district or personal ballot
applicability is invented. Public reference responses omit this internal binding.

Unbound legacy references retain their explicit-jurisdiction requirement. Existing
snapshots remain readable unchanged. New bound snapshots require the compatible
reader before import/publication and remain separate from prior-runtime cohorts
for rollback acceptance.

## Deterministic UI acceptance

Two existing resilience tests submitted real ZIP lookups to external ZIP/Census
services. A failed or incomplete provider result could leave the browser on the
home page and fail an unrelated storage/cancellation assertion. Their successful
lookup path now uses an explicit browser-network fixture, verifies the submitted
ZIP and rendered destination, and retains the storage and cancellation checks.
Provider implementations and their tests are unchanged; this fixture is not
evidence of live provider availability or production lookup success.
