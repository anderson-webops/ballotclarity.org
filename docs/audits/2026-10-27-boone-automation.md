# Boone County automatic candidate collection

## Source and scope

The [county election index](https://boonecounty.in.gov/offices/clerk/election-information/)
offers a filing workbook and official general-election sample ballots. Ordinary
public-address-pinned HTTPS collection on October 27 returned both publications,
the index, homepage and reviewed robots document without redirects or access
workarounds. The two wildcard robots groups exclude unrelated plugin metadata.
The homepage/index retain the reviewed rights notice and marriage-license
service links, not separate collection terms. New policy declarations or linked
terms require review; no blanket republication permission is claimed.

The 28,515-byte workbook has SHA-256
`a831ecef1968b29896430c1587f3cc01e1eb965eb87618c24e4c9e411000daab`.
All 86 independently reconciled filing claims retain their source rows, literal
parties and 47 office/area contexts. Eighty-five records are filed; one remains
inactive with the explicit instruction `Do not put on ballot`. Its cause is not
inferred. Filing does not establish certification or nomination. Address/contact
columns and 97 private values remain outside captures and normalized facts.

The 4,752,468-byte [sample-ballot PDF](https://boonecounty.in.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/INBOOG26_AllPcts_InfinityS-1.pdf)
has SHA-256 `cd0fdb40ff4d2873aba611f853e90601d7a647631fc6742347aa56824a74f6b2`.
Its 708 pages extract in 1,349,370 bytes; all 86 official ballot headers agree on
Boone County, Indiana, General Election, November 3, 2026. The first-page native
rendering was also inspected. The PDF establishes election context, not certified
membership of every workbook entry. No measure ingestion is claimed here.

## Reusable collection and retention

`in-boone-2026` follows actual offered workbook/sample controls. Future
`in-boone-general-YYYY-MM-DD` identities require the date printed in mutually
consistent official ballot headers, including odd-year general elections. Neither
filenames nor calendar arithmetic manufacture a future publication or date.
Missing ballot dates create no election; missing or empty validated workbooks
retain durable waiting state rather than successful zero-record imports.

The existing bounded workbook worker uses its explicit Boone profile; the
standard profile remains unchanged. Spreadsheet visibility, formulas, alternate
display content, printer-role integrity and resource limits remain checked.
Unrelated custom-XML metadata is not mistaken for hidden spreadsheet content.
The optional maintained Poppler reader uses a source-specific 1,000-page ceiling
within its existing supported range, preserving the 12-MiB input, 4-MiB output
and 12-second limits and other sources' default page ceiling.

Normalized snapshots retain both document hashes, actual retrieval clocks and
the oldest document clock as snapshot freshness. The workbook filename is not
a verified publication date. Accepted-entry loss, reversed explicit exclusions,
older captures, changed policies and inconsistent source identity require
review while retaining last-good data. Public reads use durable facts without
publisher requests or native conversion tools. Source office paths remain
unmapped; no personal ballot preview is enabled.

## Verification boundary

Actual managed collection/import retained all 86 facts, repeated unchanged
discovery without rewriting snapshots, and recovered exact records after
restart with zero public-reader publisher requests. Synthetic future, missing,
empty, conflicting and malformed publications exercise waiting and review holds;
they are not observed future candidacies. Existing Indiana records and default
Vermont workbook behavior remain separate regression checks.

Raw publications and intermediary extraction remain local, outside Git and
release assets. Source release, receiving-host collection configuration, private
data receipt and public activation are separate acceptance boundaries.
