# Johnson County candidate collection

The independent [county index](https://johnsoncountyiowa.gov/auditor/elections)
offers its [November 3, 2026 publication](https://johnsoncountyiowa.gov/november-3-2026-general-election).
Normal pinned HTTPS acquisition succeeded on October 26. The state portal's
earlier access hold is unchanged; no denied route or alternate identity is used.

## Facts and scope

The complete eight-section list supplies 102 candidate/office records: eighteen
statewide, three congressional district 1, two Senate districts 43/45, nine House
districts 85/86/89/90/91/92 and seventy local/regional. Its 42 partisan choices
retain 24 Democratic, fifteen Republican, one Libertarian and two No Party
labels. Missing parties on the other sixty remain unset. Governor/running-mate
pairs remain one ticket, and single-name offices are not inferred unopposed.

Nine explicit retention choices include three statewide Court of Appeals judges
and six unmapped regional judges. No judicial district, incumbency, nomination
or personal eligibility is inferred. Township rows contribute 43 choices with
distinct jurisdictions and printed vacancy, seat and term rules. Nine empty
office rows and the explicit district 46 not-on-ballot note create no people or
upcoming contests. Six measure sections remain separately identified factual
references, not native publication. Duplicate measure codes fail closed.

Addresses, phone numbers and protected email links are discarded. Email is not
decoded or followed; websites are not inferred from contact domains. Git fixtures
omit actual contact details while preserving all normalized facts. Raw evidence
stays local. The existing 102 Story County records remain independent and exact.

## Automation and boundaries

The `ia-johnson-2026` family reads exact dates/links actually offered by the index.
Every selected page must identify the same general election. Pages are acquired
once per discovery and reused for normalization, durable imports and cached
reads. Changed publication hashes invalidate cached facts; unchanged captures
preserve successful-import clocks. Explicit future awaiting-records pages remain
pending rather than becoming empty imports. Unknown offices, parties, term/seat
rules, dates, sections, controls and incomplete sets require review.

Synthetic 2028 date/ticket fixtures are compatibility tests, not future candidate
evidence. The actually offered 2024 page now contains results, not a candidate
list; it enables no historical import or candidate reconstruction. Source
preference requires a verified matching county and retains approximate-location,
other-area and unavailable-source fallbacks. Local candidates are not assigned
to personal ballots without independent geography. No new UI copy is added.

Robots permits the used paths. Before content requests, collection verifies its
exact fingerprint and the homepage's full legal footer/link inventory. The
reviewed home/index/general pages offer no website-reuse policy link. Copyright
is evidence, not a blanket license; only attributed facts and official question
references are normalized. Five exact reviewed driver, marriage and alcohol/
tobacco service label/URL pairs are not website-content licenses. Only those
pairs are excluded from the legal fingerprint. New licensing/policy links,
changed labels or altered footer/copyright require review.

Shared guards remain unchanged: serial eleven-second spacing, zero redirects,
2 MiB pages, 25-second timeout and normal certificate validation. Neither
collector nor reader needs a converter. Story County's merged robots groups,
Monroe's comment-only robots and unsupported machine text in retained
Independence samples remain review boundaries; no new document is collected
through those holds and no old snapshot is relabeled.

Acceptance covers actual acquisition, independent row reconciliation, all 102
compiled API records/fields, durable/cached clocks, county selection/fallbacks,
future/error fixtures and responsive pages, plus native and protected release
checks. Source delivery, authorized scheduling, data transfer/import and public
activation remain distinct. No production administration or private transfer is
included.
